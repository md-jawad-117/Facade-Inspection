What these measurements mean

Visible extent is the predicted mask’s major-axis extent on the registered wall plane. It is not the traced crack length or a validated crack-opening measurement. A change is reported only when it exceeds the sum of the two declared measurement allowances. These allowances are not calibrated statistical confidence intervals.

Structural role and material properties are unverified. Surface detections do not establish load capacity, structural severity or repair adequacy. The controlled scenario uses larger demonstration cracks; it does not validate the original 5–10 cm crack target.
